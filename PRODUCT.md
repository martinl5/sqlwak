# Product

## Register

product

## Users
SQL learners preparing for data analyst, data engineer, or business analyst interviews. Context: solo, focused practice sessions — not classroom or team use. Job to be done: get fast at writing correct SQL for the kind of business questions that appear in technical interviews.

## Product Purpose
SQLwak teaches SQL through gamified, realistic bank scenarios at Lion City Bank (Singapore). Users progress through 50+ challenges across four difficulty tiers. No signup, no backend, runs entirely in the browser. Success = users who can write window functions and CTEs confidently before their next interview.

## Brand Personality
Sharp, rewarding, immersive. The feeling of being a real analyst at a trading floor terminal — not a student doing homework. Playful through mechanics (XP, doubloons, level progression), serious through craft (clean editor, real business framing, no hand-holding after the first hint).

## Anti-references
NOT: Duolingo-style bright edtech. NOT: corporate SaaS navy-and-teal. NOT: generic dark-mode coding bootcamp. NOT: clunky LeetCode UI.

## Design Principles
1. **The terminal is the product** — the editor and query result are the primary surface; everything else supports them.
2. **Earn it** — rewards (XP, level transitions) should feel deserved, not spammy.
3. **Legibility over atmosphere** — dark and moody is fine; unreadable is not.
4. **Immersion through detail** — Singapore banking flavor, maritime metaphors, and precise copy make it feel like a real place.
5. **No hand-holding after the first hint** — the interface trusts the user.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA: 4.5:1 body text contrast, keyboard navigable, screen-reader-compatible semantic HTML. Reduced motion alternatives for all keyframe animations.
